Get weather alerts by text message

wifieye, Inc. monitors weather conditions at job sites and sends automated text alerts to the staff and site contacts who ask for them, for example when the temperature, humidity or air quality at a site crosses a set limit.

To sign up, text YES to +1 559-238-8812

After you text YES, you will receive an automatic confirmation message. Alerts start once your number has been added to your site's alert list.

What you will receive

Automated weather and station alerts for your site, such as:

"wifieye weather alert -

[Site Name]:

Temperature is above 72.2 °F

Currently at 72.3 °F on Oct 13, 2:12 PM

Reply STOP to opt out."

Details

  • Message frequency varies with weather conditions.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. Reply HELP for help, or email support@wifieye.com.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
