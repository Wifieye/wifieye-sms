SMS Terms

Program description. wifieye, Inc. sends automated weather and equipment alerts by text message, for example when a site's temperature, humidity, air quality or pressure crosses a limit, or when a weather station stops reporting. The messages are informational and are sent only to people who have asked to receive them.

How you join. A wifieye, Inc. administrator adds your email address and mobile number to a job site's contact list and we email you an invitation for each job site you are on. To join, text YES to +1 559-238-8812, as shown on our sign-up page. If you are on more than one job site, text YES followed by the job site name shown in that job site's invitation email (for example YES VISALIA), once for each job site you want alerts from. You will receive a confirmation message. No alerts are sent for a job site until you have texted to sign up for it. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency. Message frequency varies with weather conditions and station status.

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge for messages and data according to your plan.

Opt out. Reply STOP at any time to cancel all alerts. You will receive one final message confirming that you are unsubscribed, and no more messages will be sent unless you ask to rejoin. To cancel just one job site, reply STOP followed by the job site name (for example STOP VISALIA); you will keep the alerts for your other job sites.

Help. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@wifieye.com.
