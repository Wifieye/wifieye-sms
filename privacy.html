Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wifieye Inc ("we", "us") handles personal information in connection with our weather monitoring and alert service, including the automated text messages we send to people who have asked to receive them.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, which you give us so that we can send you alerts for a site you are connected to.
  • Your reply to our confirmation text (for example "Y"), including the date and time, which we keep as a record of your consent.
  • Records of the alert messages we send to you and whether they were delivered.

How we use it

We use this information only to send you the automated weather alerts you asked for, to confirm and keep a record of your consent, and to respond to your questions. The messages contain weather and station readings only. We do not collect other personal information from you through these messages, and we do not use it for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is shared only with the service providers that deliver our messages (such as our messaging and telephone carriers), and only so that they can deliver messages to you, or when the law requires it. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party for their own marketing.

Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as you are subscribed to alerts and for a reasonable period afterward to keep our consent records. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorized access.

Your choices

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us at the address below.
